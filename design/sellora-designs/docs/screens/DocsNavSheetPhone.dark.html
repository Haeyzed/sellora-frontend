Assign deliveries to your drivers

Send an order out with one of your own drivers, follow it on the road and see the cash they collect.

Available on Growth and Pro 5 min read·Updated 12 Nov 2026
On this page
  • Assign an order
  • What each status means
  • If a driver is locked out
Before you start. Add at least one driver under Delivery › Drivers and give them their 8-digit setup code. Drivers sign in on the Sellora Driver app with their phone number and a PIN.

Assign an order

  1. 1Open the order. Go to Orders and open an order that is paid, or set to cash on delivery, and ready to ship.
  2. 2Choose Assign delivery. Pick a driver who is active. Drivers who are locked out or inactive are not listed.
  3. 3Add a note if needed. For example a gate code or a landmark. The driver sees it on the job.
  4. 4Follow it on the Delivery page. The status updates as the driver picks up, sets off and delivers.

What each status means

StatusWhat it meansWho moves it

FailedCould not deliver; you can retry or return itDriver
Cash on delivery. The order is only marked paid when you record the driver's cash handover, not when the driver marks it delivered.

If a driver is locked out

After 5 wrong PINs the driver app locks. Open the driver in Delivery › Drivers and choose Unlock with a new setup code. Give them the new code; they choose a new PIN.
